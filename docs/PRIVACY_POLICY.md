# Privacy Policy for Student Intelligent System

**Last Updated:** November 27, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Student Intelligent System ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our educational management application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **User Credentials:** Username, email address, and password (encrypted)
- **User Type:** Admin, Teacher, Student, or Parent
- **Profile Information:** 
  - Name and contact details
  - Grade/Class information (for students and teachers)
  - Parent-student relationships
  - Teacher-subject assignments

### Academic Data
- **Attendance Records:** Date, time, status (Present/Absent), and marking teacher
- **Subject Information:** Subject names, codes, and assigned teachers
- **Academic Results:** Grades, scores, exam types, and performance metrics
- **Announcements:** Educational notices and communications

### Automatically Collected Information
- Device information and operating system details
- App usage data and error logs
- Login timestamps and session information
- Synchronization logs

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- **Authentication & Access Control:** Verify user identity and manage role-based permissions
- **Academic Management:**
  - Track student attendance and generate reports
  - Record and display academic results
  - Manage subjects and teacher assignments
  - Facilitate parent-teacher-student communication
- **Data Synchronization:** Sync data across devices for authorized users via Supabase
- **Analytics:** Monitor student performance trends and attendance patterns
- **System Improvement:** Identify and fix bugs, improve app functionality
- **Communication:** Send announcements and important notifications

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database
- Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing algorithms before storage
- Local database is protected by device-level security

### Cloud Storage (Supabase)
- User credentials and academic data are synchronized with Supabase cloud database
- All data transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols
- We implement Row Level Security (RLS) policies to protect data access
- Foreign key constraints maintain data integrity and relationships
- Access to cloud data requires authentication tokens

### Security Measures
- Password encryption with secure hashing
- Secure API communication with authentication tokens
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Regular security audits of database policies
- Automatic session timeout for inactive users

## Data Sharing

### We DO NOT:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers or marketing companies
- Use your information for purposes other than educational management
- Provide student data to unauthorized third parties

### We MAY Share Data:
- **Within Your Institution:** With authorized teachers, administrators, and parents as appropriate
- **With Parents:** Student-specific attendance and academic performance data
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, court order, or legal process
- **Safety:** To protect the safety of users or the public

## User Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View your personal data stored in the system
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Export:** Download your data in a portable format
- **Opt-Out:** Choose local-only mode to prevent cloud synchronization
- **Restrict Processing:** Limit how your data is used

### Exercising Your Rights
Contact your institution's administrator or email [Your Contact Email] to exercise these rights.

## Data Retention

### Active Accounts
- Data is retained while your account is active and in use
- Attendance and academic records maintained for institutional record-keeping

### Inactive/Deleted Accounts
- Deleted accounts and personal data are permanently removed within 30 days
- Academic records may be retained for legal or institutional requirements
- Anonymized data may be retained for statistical purposes

## Children's Privacy

This app is designed for educational institutions and may be used by students under 13 years of age.

### Compliance
- We comply with applicable children's privacy laws (COPPA, GDPR, etc.)
- Parental consent and institutional authorization required for students under 13
- Parents have the right to review and request deletion of their child's data
- Minimal data collection principle applied for all student users

### Parental Controls
Parents can:
- Access their child's academic data through the parent portal
- Request data deletion or correction
- Opt out of non-essential data collection
